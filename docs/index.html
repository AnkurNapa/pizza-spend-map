How the score works

Demand is the average percentile, across all 347 areas, of population density plus the shares of people aged 16 to 34, full-time students, households with children, one-person households, flats and renters.

Spend is 60% the percentile of net household income (adjusted by the regional takeaway multiplier from ONS Family Spending) plus 40% the inverse percentile of income deprivation.

Gap is 100 minus the percentile of pizza outlets per 1,000 residents. Outlets with a hygiene rating of 0 to 2 count as half a competitor. Outlets just over an area boundary are not counted, so treat border areas with care.

Opportunity is the weighted average of the three. The sliders change the weights; the sub-scores stay fixed.

School zones are shown, not scored. Since August 2026 national policy (NPPF HC5) says new takeaways should be refused within about 400 m of schools unless the site is in a designated town centre. Red rings mark councils with their own rule as well. Only secondary and all-through schools are drawn, but the London Plan and some councils also cover primary schools. Check a site against the council's policy before you sign a lease.
